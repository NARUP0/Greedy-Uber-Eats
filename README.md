# 🍔 Greedy Uber Eats — Data Analysis

Data cleaning and exploratory analysis of Uber Eats food delivery orders (in INR), converting raw CSV records into a structured Excel (`.xlsx`) workbook to guide restaurant placement and revenue planning.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview
The dataset includes **35 columns** covering 5 key business areas:
- **Order & Time Details:** Order tracking, dates, peak hours, meal periods, and weekday vs. weekend indicators.
- **Customer Profile:** Customer demographics, new vs. returning status, ordering devices, and acquisition channels.
- **Restaurant & Menu:** Cities, neighborhoods, restaurant partners, cuisines, ordered items, and quantities.
- **Financials (INR):** Item pricing, gross and final order values, discounts, promo codes, fees, taxes, payouts, platform revenue, and net profit.
- **Logistics & Experience:** Delivery duration, travel distance, weather conditions, and customer ratings.

---

## ⚙️ Key Analyses Performed
- **Data Cleaning & Conversion:** Converted raw `.csv` to `.xlsx`, removed duplicates, handled missing values, and standardized date, time, and INR formats.
- **Area & Location Analysis:** Identified high-revenue cities and neighborhoods to determine optimal locations for opening new restaurants or cloud kitchens.
- **Time-Sensitive Demand Analysis:** Compared weekday vs. weekend order volume and meal periods to evaluate steady daily revenue against irregular weekend spikes for staffing and inventory planning.
